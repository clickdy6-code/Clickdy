Who we are

Clickdy ("Clickdy", "we", "us") runs the website at clickdy.vercel.app, a collection of free online tools and a tech and AI news blog. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use our site, how it is used, and the choices you have.

By using Clickdy, you agree to this policy. If you don't agree with it, please don't use the site.

Information we handle

Files and text you use in our tools

Most of our tools (such as PDF, image, text and QR tools) are built to run directly in your browser. The files and text you use in those tools are processed on your own device, and we don't receive or store them.

Our AI tools may send the text or images you provide to a third-party AI service so it can create a result for you. Please don't enter sensitive personal information, passwords or confidential documents into AI tools.

Messages you send us

If you use our contact form, we receive the name, email address, topic and message you type. The message is delivered to our email inbox through a third-party form service. We use this information only to read and reply to your message.

Technical information collected automatically

When you visit any website, certain technical information is sent automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you view, the date and time, and the page you came from. Our hosting provider and our advertising partners may receive this information. We don't currently use website analytics tools, and we don't build visitor profiles ourselves.

What we don't ask for

Because there are no accounts on Clickdy, we don't ask for your name, password or payment details to use our tools.

Advertising

Clickdy is free to use and is supported by advertising. Ads on our site are shown by third-party advertising networks.

These partners may use cookies, device identifiers, your IP address and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure how ads perform, detect fraud and, depending on where you live and your settings, show ads based on your interests. We don't control these technologies, and we don't receive a personal profile of you from them. Their own privacy policies explain how they handle information.

Your choices. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can use your device's ad personalization settings. You can also opt out of interest-based ads from many partners using these tools: Digital Advertising Alliance (US), Network Advertising Initiative and Your Online Choices (Europe). Opting out doesn't remove ads. It only makes them less personalized.

Where the law requires it, our advertising partners may ask for your consent before using cookies for ads.

Cookies and similar technologies

Clickdy doesn't need cookies to work, because there are no logins or accounts. The cookies you may see while using our pages mainly come from our advertising partners and from our hosting and security providers.

Some tools may also store small amounts of data in your browser, for example to remember your work. That data stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser data. If you block cookies, the site will still work, but some ads may not load as expected.

How we use information

  • To run the site and provide our tools.
  • To reply to messages, questions and feedback.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To show ads through our advertising partners, which helps keep Clickdy free.
  • To fix problems and decide which tools to improve or build next.
  • To follow the law when we are required to.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run Clickdy:

  • Hosting and security providers that deliver the website to you.
  • A form service that delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • AI service providers that process the prompts you submit to AI tools.
  • Advertising partners that show ads on our site, as described above.

We don't sell the information you send us through the contact form. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of Clickdy and its visitors. If Clickdy is ever transferred to someone else, information may be transferred with it.

How long we keep it

Files and text you process in your browser are not stored by us. Contact messages are kept for as long as we need to respond and keep reasonable records, and then deleted. Third parties, such as advertising partners and AI services, keep information according to their own policies.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle, and our pages are served over a secure connection. However, no website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children

Clickdy is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain uses, to withdraw consent you have given, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we collect very little, most requests will relate to a message you sent through the contact form. To make a request, contact us. We may need to confirm it's really you before we act on it. For information held by advertising partners and other third parties, please use the choices described in the Advertising section or contact those companies directly.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Links to other sites

Our blog articles link to the original sources they are based on, and ads link to advertisers' websites. We aren't responsible for the content or privacy practices of other websites, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we add a new tool or change how the site works. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Please check back now and then.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please get in touch.
