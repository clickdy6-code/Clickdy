Blue Planet III keeps rough shots to prove authenticity

The BBC's upcoming nature series Blue Planet III will feature less-than-perfect shots to counter overly polished visuals associated with AI.

25 Oct 20261 min readClickdy News

The upcoming BBC nature series Blue Planet III is taking steps to demonstrate its authenticity by intentionally keeping less-than-perfect shots in the final cut. Past entries in the franchise were well known for their stunning, crystal-clear footage that sometimes appeared almost too good to be true.

As artificial intelligence makes it easier to generate seamless and highly polished visuals, overly perfect footage has increasingly become a dead giveaway for synthetic content. To counter this trend and ensure viewers trust the reality of the footage, the production team has chosen to retain the rough edges and imperfections that naturally occur in wildlife filmmaking.
