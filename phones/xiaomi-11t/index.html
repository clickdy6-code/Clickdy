Xiaomi 11T at a glance

The Xiaomi 11T was announced in September 2021. It has a 6.67-inch OLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 1200 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the 11T: MediaTek Dimensity 1200-Ultra, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 120W wired charging.

The phone weighs 203 g, measures 8.8 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 74 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
