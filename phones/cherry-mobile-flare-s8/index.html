Cherry Mobile Flare S8 at a glance

The Cherry Mobile Flare S8 was announced in July 2019. It has a 6.26-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the S8/S8 Lite/S8 Prime/S8 Max: Mediatek Helio P23, with up to 6 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 13 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 13 MP.

A 2800 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone runs Android 9.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 42 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2800 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 16 GB
