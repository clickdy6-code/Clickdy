Motorola Moto E4 at a glance

The Motorola Moto E4 is a smartphone. It has a 5-inch display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 427, with up to 3 GB of RAM and up to 32 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 8 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 5 MP.

A 2800 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 198 g, measures 9.3 mm in thickness and runs Android 7.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 30 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2800 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 16 GB
  • Only 3 GB RAM
  • Thicker than most phones (9.3 mm)
