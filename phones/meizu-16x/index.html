Meizu 16X at a glance

The Meizu 16X is a smartphone. It has a 6-inch Super AMOLED display.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 2 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 20 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 3100 mAh battery keeps it running, with 18W wired charging.

The phone weighs 154 g and runs Android 8.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 48 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Highlights

Things to consider
