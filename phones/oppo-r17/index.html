OPPO R17 at a glance

The OPPO R17 was announced in August 2018. It has a 6.4-inch AMOLED display with a 60 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the R17:, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 16 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 25 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 3500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 50W wired charging.

The phone weighs 182 g, measures 7.5 mm in thickness and runs Android 8.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 56 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
