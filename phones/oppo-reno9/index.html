Oppo Reno9 at a glance

The Oppo Reno9 is a smartphone. It has a 6.7-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Reno9: Qualcomm SM7325 Snapdragon 778G 5G, with up to 56 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 64 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 80W wired charging.

The phone weighs 174 g and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 79 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
