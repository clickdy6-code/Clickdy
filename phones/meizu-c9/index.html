Meizu C9 at a glance

The Meizu C9 was announced in December 2018. It has a 5.45-inch LCD display. It is powered by the UNISOC SC9832E, with up to 3 GB of RAM and up to 32 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 13 MP main camera. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 3000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 5W wired charging.

The phone weighs 150 g and runs Android 8.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 31 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (3000 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 16 GB
