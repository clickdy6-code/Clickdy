Lenovo Moto G54 5G at a glance

The Lenovo Moto G54 5G was announced in September 2023. It is powered by the MediaTeK MT6855, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization. The front camera is 16 MP.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 33W wired charging.

The phone weighs 177 g, measures 8 mm in thickness and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 75 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
