Motorola Moto Z3 Play at a glance

The Motorola Moto Z3 Play is a smartphone. It has a 6.01-inch Super AMOLED display.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 3000 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 156 g, measures 6.75 mm in thickness and runs Android 8.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 47 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • Battery capacity is on the small side (3000 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
