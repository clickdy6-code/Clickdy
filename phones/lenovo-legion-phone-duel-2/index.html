Lenovo Legion Phone Duel 2 at a glance

The Lenovo Legion Phone Duel 2 was announced in April 2021. It has a 6.92-inch AMOLED display with a 144 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 888, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 64 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 44 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K@24fps.

A 5500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 90W wired charging.

The phone weighs 259 g and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 81 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
