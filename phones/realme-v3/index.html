Realme V3 at a glance

The Realme V3 was announced in September 2020. It has a 6.5-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the Mediatek Dimensity 720, with up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 13 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 18W wired charging.

The phone runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 53 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
