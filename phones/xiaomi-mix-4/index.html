Xiaomi MIX 4 at a glance

The Xiaomi MIX 4 was announced in August 2021. It has a 6.67-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 888+, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 12 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 108 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 20 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K@24fps.

A 4500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 120W wired and 50W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 225 g and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 82 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
