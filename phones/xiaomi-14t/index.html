Xiaomi 14T at a glance

The Xiaomi 14T was announced in September 2024. It has a 6.67-inch display with a 144 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the 14T: MediaTek Dimensity 8300 Ultra, with up to 24 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 50W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 195 g, measures 7.8 mm in thickness and runs Android 14.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 86 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
