Oppo Reno5 Lite at a glance

The Oppo Reno5 Lite was announced in March 2021. It has a 6.43-inch AMOLED display.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 4 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4310 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 172 g and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 69 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
