Transsion (on contract) Infinix Note 60 Pro at a glance

The Transsion (on contract) Infinix Note 60 Pro was announced in March 2026. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7s Gen 4, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.
