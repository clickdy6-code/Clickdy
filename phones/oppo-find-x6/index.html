Oppo Find X6 at a glance

The Oppo Find X6 was announced in March 2023. It has a 6.74-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Find X6: List of MediaTek systems on chips#Dimensity 9000 Se, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4800 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 207 g and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 82 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
