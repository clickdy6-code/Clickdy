Vivo Y90 at a glance

The Vivo Y90 was announced in July 2019. It has a 6.22-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the Mediatek MT6761 Helio A22, with up to 2 GB of RAM and up to 32 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 8 MP main camera. The front camera is 5 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 4030 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone runs Android 8.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 34 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (4030 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 16 GB
  • Only 2 GB RAM
