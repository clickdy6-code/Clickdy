Sony Xperia T3 at a glance

The Sony Xperia T3 is a smartphone. It has a 5.3-inch display. It is powered by the Qualcomm MSM8928-2 Snapdragon 400, with up to 1 GB of RAM and up to 8 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 8 MP main camera. The front camera is 1.1 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p.

A 2500 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone runs Android 4.4.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 31 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2500 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 8 GB
  • Only 1 GB RAM
