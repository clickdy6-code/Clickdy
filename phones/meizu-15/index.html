Meizu 15 at a glance

The Meizu 15 was announced in April 2018. It has a 5.46-inch Super AMOLED display. It is powered by the 15: Qualcomm SDM660 Snapdragon 660, with up to 6 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 20 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 3000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 24W wired charging.

The phone weighs 152 g, measures 7.45 mm in thickness and runs Android 7.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 47 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
