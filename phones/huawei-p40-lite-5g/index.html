Huawei P40 lite 5G at a glance

The Huawei P40 lite 5G was announced in April 2020. It has a 6.5-inch IPS LCD display.

On the back, it offers a 64 MP main camera and 4 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 40W wired charging.

The phone weighs 189 g, measures 8.6 mm in thickness and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 67 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
