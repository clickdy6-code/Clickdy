Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 was announced in September 2025. It is powered by the Tab A11: MediaTek Helio G99, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

A 5100 mAh battery keeps it running, with 25W wired charging.

The phone runs Android 15.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 57 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
