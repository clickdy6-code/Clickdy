OnePlus One at a glance

The OnePlus One was announced in April 2014. It has a 5.46-inch IPS display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 801 MSM8974PRO-AC r2p1, with up to 3 GB of RAM and up to 64 GB of storage.

A 3100 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 162 g, measures 8.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 4.4.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 28 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (3100 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 16 GB
  • Only 3 GB RAM
