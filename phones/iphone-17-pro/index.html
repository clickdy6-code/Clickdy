IPhone 17 Pro at a glance

The IPhone 17 Pro was announced in September 2025. It has a 6.3-inch display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 3000 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Apple A19 Pro, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 2048 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 18 MP.

A 3988 mAh battery keeps it running, with 40W wired charging.

The phone weighs 206 g and runs iOS 26.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 82 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
