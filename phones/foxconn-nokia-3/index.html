Foxconn Nokia 3 at a glance

The Foxconn Nokia 3 was announced in June 2017. It has a 5-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the MediaTek MT6737, with up to 2 GB of RAM and up to 16 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 8 MP main camera. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 720p.

A 2630 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 140 g, measures 8.48 mm in thickness and runs Android 7.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 26 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2630 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 16 GB
  • Only 2 GB RAM
