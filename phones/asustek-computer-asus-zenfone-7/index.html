AsusTek Computer Asus ZenFone 7 at a glance

The AsusTek Computer Asus ZenFone 7 was announced in September 2020. It has a 6.67-inch Super AMOLED display with a 90 Hz refresh rate and up to 700 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 865, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 64 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. Video recording goes up to 8K.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 30W wired charging.

The phone weighs 230 g, measures 9.6 mm in thickness and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 74 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
