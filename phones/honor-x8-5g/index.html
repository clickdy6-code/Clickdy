Honor X8 5G at a glance

The Honor X8 5G was announced in May 2022. It has a 6.5-inch TFT display with a 90 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm SM4350-AC Snapdragon 480+, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 4 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 22.5W wired charging.

The phone weighs 194 g and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 61 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
