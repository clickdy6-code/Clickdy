Samsung Galaxy Fold at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy Fold was announced in September 2019. It has a 7.3-inch Dynamic AMOLED display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 855, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

A 4380 mAh battery keeps it running, with 15W wired and 15W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 263 g, measures 6.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 9.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 71 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
