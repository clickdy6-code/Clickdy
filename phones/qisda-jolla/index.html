Qisda Jolla at a glance

The Qisda Jolla was announced in November 2013. It has a 4.5-inch IPS display.

A 2100 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 141 g and measures 9.9 mm in thickness.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 25 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
