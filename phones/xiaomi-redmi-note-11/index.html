Xiaomi Redmi Note 11 at a glance

The Xiaomi Redmi Note 11 was announced in January 2022. It has a 6.43-inch display. It is powered by the Note 11: Qualcomm Snapdragon 680 4G, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 13 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 120W wired charging.

The phone weighs 179 g, measures 8.1 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 70 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
