Foxconn Pixel 5a at a glance

The Foxconn Pixel 5a was announced in August 2021. It has a 6.34-inch OLED display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 765G, with up to 6 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12.2 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4680 mAh battery keeps it running, with 18W wired charging.

The phone weighs 183 g, measures 7.6 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 62 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
