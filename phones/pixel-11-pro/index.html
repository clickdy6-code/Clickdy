Pixel 11 Pro at a glance

The Pixel 11 Pro was announced in August 2026. It is powered by the Google Tensor G6, with up to 56 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 42 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p.

A 4850 mAh battery keeps it running, with 45W wired and 25W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 204 g, measures 8.4 mm in thickness and runs Android 17.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 91 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
