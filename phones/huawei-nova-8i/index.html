Huawei Nova 8i at a glance

The Huawei Nova 8i is a smartphone. It has a 6.67-inch IPS LCD display with a 120 Hz refresh rate.

On the back, it offers a 64 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 4300 mAh battery keeps it running, with 66W wired charging.

The phone weighs 190 g and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 70 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
