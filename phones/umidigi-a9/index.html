Umidigi A9 at a glance

The Umidigi A9 is a smartphone. It has a 6.3-inch display. It is powered by the MediaTek Helio G25, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 24 MP.

A 4150 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone measures 8.6 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 55 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (4150 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 64 GB
