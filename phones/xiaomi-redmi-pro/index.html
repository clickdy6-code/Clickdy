Xiaomi Redmi Pro at a glance

The Xiaomi Redmi Pro was announced in July 2016. It has a 5.5-inch AMOLED display. It is powered by the Redmi Pro: MediaTek MT6797 Helio X20, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 13 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 5 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 4050 mAh battery keeps it running, with 10W wired charging.

The phone weighs 174 g and runs Android 6.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 44 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (4050 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
