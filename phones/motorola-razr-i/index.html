Motorola RAZR i at a glance

The Motorola RAZR i was announced in September 2012. It is powered by the Intel Atom Z2480 Medfield, with up to 1 GB of RAM and up to 8 GB of storage.

A 2000 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone runs Android 4.4.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 37 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2000 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 8 GB
  • Only 1 GB RAM
