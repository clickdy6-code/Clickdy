Vivo X Flip at a glance

The Vivo X Flip was announced in April 2023. It has a 6.74-inch LTPO AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4400 mAh battery keeps it running, with 44W wired charging.

The phone weighs 198 g and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 77 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
