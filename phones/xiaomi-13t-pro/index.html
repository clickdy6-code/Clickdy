Xiaomi 13T Pro at a glance

The Xiaomi 13T Pro was announced in September 2023. It has a 6.67-inch AMOLED display with a 144 Hz refresh rate and up to 2600 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Mediatek Dimensity 9200+, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 120W wired charging.

The phone weighs 200 g and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 86 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
