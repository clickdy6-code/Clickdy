Huawei Nova 12i at a glance

The Huawei Nova 12i is a smartphone. It has a 6.7-inch IPS LCD display with a 90 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Nova 12i/13i/Enjoy 70 Pro: Qualcomm SM6225 Snapdragon 680 4G, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 108 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 40W wired charging.

The phone weighs 199 g, measures 8.4 mm in thickness and runs Android 12.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 64 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
