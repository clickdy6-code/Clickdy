Foxconn Pixel 4a at a glance

The Foxconn Pixel 4a was announced in August 2020. It has a 5.8-inch OLED display. It is powered by the Pixel 4a: Qualcomm Qualcomm Snapdragon, with up to 6 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12.2 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K.

A 3140 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 143 g, measures 8.2 mm in thickness and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 52 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
