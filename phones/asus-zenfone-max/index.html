Asus ZenFone Max at a glance

The Asus ZenFone Max is a smartphone. It has a 5.45-inch IPS display. It is powered by the Max, with up to 6 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 13 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 4000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 10W wired charging.

The phone weighs 150 g, measures 8.7 mm in thickness and runs Android 7.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 45 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (4000 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
