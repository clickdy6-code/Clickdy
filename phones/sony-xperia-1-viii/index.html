Sony Xperia 1 VIII at a glance

The Sony Xperia 1 VIII was announced in June 2026. It has a 6.5-inch LTPO FHD+ 19.5:9 (2340 x 1080) HDR OLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 52 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 12 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@24fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 30W wired and 15W wireless charging.

The phone has an IP65 rating, weighs 200 g, measures 8.3 mm in thickness and runs Android 16.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 88 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
