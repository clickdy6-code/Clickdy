OnePlus 8T at a glance

The OnePlus 8T was announced in October 2020. It has a 6.55-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 865, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 4 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 65W wired charging.

The phone weighs 188 g, measures 8.4 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 72 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
