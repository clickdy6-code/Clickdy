Oppo Realme C25 at a glance

The Oppo Realme C25 was announced in February 2021. It has a 6.5-inch IPS LCD display.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 6000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 18W wired charging.

The phone weighs 209 g and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 59 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
