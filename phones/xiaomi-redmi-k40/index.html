Xiaomi Redmi K40 at a glance

The Xiaomi Redmi K40 was announced in March 2021. It has a 6.67-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the K40/40S: Qualcomm List of Qualcomm Snapdragon processors#Sna, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 64 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 20 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4520 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 196 g, measures 7.7 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 79 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
