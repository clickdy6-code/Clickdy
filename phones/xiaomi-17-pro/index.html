Xiaomi 17 Pro at a glance

The Xiaomi 17 Pro was announced in September 2025. It has a 6.3-inch LTPO AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 3500 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 50 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K@30fps.

A 6300 mAh battery keeps it running, with 100W wired and 50W wireless charging.

The phone measures 162.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 16.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 93 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
