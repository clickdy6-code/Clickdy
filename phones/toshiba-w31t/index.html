Toshiba W31T at a glance

The Toshiba W31T was announced in June 2005. It has a 2.4-inch TFT display.

On the back, it offers a 2.36 MP main camera.

The phone weighs 116 g and measures 20 mm in thickness.

Things to consider

  • Thicker than most phones (20 mm)
