LG Q61 at a glance

The LG Q61 was announced in May 2020. It has a 6.53-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the MediaTek MT6765 Helio P35, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 64 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 4 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP.

A 4000 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone measures 8.3 mm in thickness and runs Android 9.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 50 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (4000 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 64 GB
  • Only 4 GB RAM
