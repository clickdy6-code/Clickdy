Oppo Find N6 at a glance

The Oppo Find N6 is a smartphone. It has a 8.12-inch display. It is powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 Mobile Platform, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 200 MP main camera and 4 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 20 MP.

A 6000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 80W wired charging.

The phone weighs 225 g.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 93 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
