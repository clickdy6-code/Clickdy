Pixel 10 Pro Fold at a glance

The Pixel 10 Pro Fold was announced in October 2025. It is powered by the Google Tensor G5, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 10 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K.

A 5015 mAh battery keeps it running, with 30W wired charging.

The phone weighs 258 g, measures 10.8 mm in thickness and runs Android 16.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 86 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
