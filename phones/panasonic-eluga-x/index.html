Panasonic Eluga X at a glance

The Panasonic Eluga X was announced in January 2013. It has a 5-inch TFT display.

On the back, it offers a 13.2 MP main camera. The front camera is 1.3 MP.

A 2320 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 152 g, measures 9.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 4.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 27 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2320 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
  • Only 2 GB RAM
