Xiaomi Mi 10 at a glance

The Xiaomi Mi 10 was announced in February 2020. It has a 6.67-inch Super AMOLED display with a 90 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 865, with up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 108 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 20 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 4780 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 208 g, measures 9 mm in thickness and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 75 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
