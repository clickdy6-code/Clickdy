Vivo X300 Ultra at a glance

The Vivo X300 Ultra was announced in April 2026. It has a 6.82-inch LTPO AMOLED display with a 144 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 200 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 4 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 50 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K@30fps.

A 6600 mAh battery keeps it running, with 100W wired and 40W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 232 g and runs Android 16.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 94 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
