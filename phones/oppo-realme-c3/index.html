Oppo Realme C3 at a glance

The Oppo Realme C3 was announced in February 2020. It is powered by the C3 & C3i: MediaTek Helio G70, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 64 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 5 MP.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 195 g and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 50 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • Base storage is only 32 GB
  • Only 4 GB RAM
