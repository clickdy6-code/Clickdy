Sony Xperia 1 IV at a glance

The Sony Xperia 1 IV was announced in June 2022. It has a 6.5-inch OLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 1, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 4 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 12 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@24fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 30W wired and 11W wireless charging.

The phone has an IP65 rating, weighs 185 g, measures 8.2 mm in thickness and runs Android 12.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 77 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
