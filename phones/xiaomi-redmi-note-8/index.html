Xiaomi Redmi Note 8 at a glance

The Xiaomi Redmi Note 8 was announced in August 2019. It has a 6.3-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the Note 8/8T: Qualcomm Snapdragon 665.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 13 MP.

A 4000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 18W wired charging.

The phone weighs 190 g, measures 8.8 mm in thickness and runs Android 9.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 53 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (4000 mAh)
