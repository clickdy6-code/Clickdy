OPPO OnePlus Nord CE4 Lite at a glance

The OPPO OnePlus Nord CE4 Lite was announced in March 2024. It has a 6.67-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm SM6375 Snapdragon 695, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 5110 mAh battery keeps it running, with 80W wired charging.

The phone weighs 191 g and runs Android 14.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 77 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
