Nubia Z20 at a glance

The Nubia Z20 was announced in August 2019. It has a 6.42-inch AMOLED display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 855+, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 8 MP main camera. Video recording goes up to 720p.

A 4000 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 186 g, measures 9.2 mm in thickness and runs Android 9.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 52 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (4000 mAh)
  • Thicker than most phones (9.2 mm)
