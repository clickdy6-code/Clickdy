Foxconn Pixel 6a at a glance

The Foxconn Pixel 6a was announced in July 2022. It has a 6.1-inch OLED display. It is powered by the Google Tensor, with up to 6 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

A 4410 mAh battery keeps it running, with 18W wired charging.

The phone weighs 178 g, measures 8.7 mm in thickness and runs Android 12.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 68 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
