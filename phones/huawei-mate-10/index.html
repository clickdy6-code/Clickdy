Huawei Mate 10 at a glance

The Huawei Mate 10 was announced in October 2017. It is powered by the HiSilicon Kirin 970, with up to 6 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 20 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K.

A 4000 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 186 g and runs Android 10.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 55 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Highlights

  • Optical image stabilization

Things to consider

  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (4000 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 64 GB
