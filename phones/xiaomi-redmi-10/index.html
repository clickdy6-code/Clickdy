Xiaomi Redmi 10 at a glance

The Xiaomi Redmi 10 was announced in August 2021. It has a 6.5-inch LCD display with a 90 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the MediaTek Helio G88, with up to 6 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 18W wired charging.

The phone weighs 181 g, measures 8.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 56 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Base storage is only 64 GB
