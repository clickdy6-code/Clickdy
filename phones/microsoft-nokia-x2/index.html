Microsoft Nokia X2 at a glance

The Microsoft Nokia X2 was announced in June 2014. It has a 4.3-inch LCD display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 MSM8926, with up to 1 GB of RAM and up to 4 GB of storage.

A 1800 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 150 g, measures 11.1 mm in thickness and runs Android 4.3.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 27 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (1800 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 4 GB
