Samsung Galaxy M14 5G at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy M14 5G is a smartphone. It has a 6.6-inch display with a 90 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Exynos 1330, with up to 6 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 13 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 6000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 25W wired charging.

The phone weighs 206 g, measures 9.4 mm in thickness and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 64 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
