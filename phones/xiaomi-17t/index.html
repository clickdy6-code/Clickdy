Xiaomi 17T at a glance

The Xiaomi 17T was announced in May 2026. It has a 6.67-inch AMOLED display with a 144 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the 17T: Mediatek Dimensity 8500 Ultra, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K@24fps.

A 6500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 100W wired charging.

The phone weighs 200 g and runs Android 16.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 92 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
