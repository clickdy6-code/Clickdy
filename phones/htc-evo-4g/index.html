HTC Evo 4G+ at a glance

The HTC Evo 4G+ is a smartphone. It has a 4.3-inch LCD display.

A 1730 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 160 g, measures 12.05 mm in thickness and runs Android 2.3.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 23 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (1730 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 4 GB
