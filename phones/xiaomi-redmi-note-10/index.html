Xiaomi Redmi Note 10 at a glance

The Xiaomi Redmi Note 10 was announced in March 2021. It has a 6.43-inch Super AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 1200 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Note 10: Qualcomm Snapdragon 678.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 13 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 67W wired charging.

The phone weighs 178.8 g, measures 8.1 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 67 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
