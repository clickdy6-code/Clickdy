Vivo T1 at a glance

The Vivo T1 was announced in October 2021. It has a 6.67-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the T1, with up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 64 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 193 g, measures 8.3 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 72 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No NFC for contactless payments
