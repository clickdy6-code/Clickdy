Pixel 2 at a glance

The Pixel 2 was announced in October 2017. It has a 5-inch AMOLED display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 835, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12.2 MP main camera with optical stabilization. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 720p.

A 2700 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 143 g and runs Android 8.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 38 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Highlights

  • Optical image stabilization

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2700 mAh)
  • Only 4 GB RAM
