Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 was announced in July 2025. It has a 8-inch LTPO AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 2600 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Qualcomm SM8750-AC Snapdragon 8 Elite, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 200 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 10 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K@30fps.

A 4400 mAh battery keeps it running, with 25W wired and 15W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 215 g, measures 8.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 16.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 84 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
