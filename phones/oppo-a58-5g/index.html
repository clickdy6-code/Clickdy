OPPO A58 5G at a glance

The OPPO A58 5G was announced in November 2022. It has a 6.56-inch IPS LCD display with a 90 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 700, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 33W wired charging.

The phone weighs 188 g, measures 8 mm in thickness and runs Android 12.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 62 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
