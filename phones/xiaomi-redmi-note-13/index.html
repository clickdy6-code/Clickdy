Xiaomi Redmi Note 13 at a glance

The Xiaomi Redmi Note 13 was announced in September 2023. It has a 6.67-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Redmi Note 13: Qualcomm Snapdragon 685, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 120W wired charging.

The phone weighs 188.5 g, measures 8 mm in thickness and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 72 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
