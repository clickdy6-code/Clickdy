Oppo Pad Neo at a glance

The Oppo Pad Neo was announced in January 2024. It is powered by the Mediatek Helio G99, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 8 MP main camera. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 8000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 33W wired charging.

The phone weighs 538 g, measures 6.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 51 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
