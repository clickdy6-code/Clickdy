Samsung Galaxy Watch 9 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy Watch 9 was announced in August 2026. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon Wear Elite, with up to 2 GB of RAM and up to 64 GB of storage.

A 390 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 31.5 g and measures 44 mm in thickness.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 40 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Highlights

  • Very bright display (5000 nits peak)

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (390 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
  • Only 2 GB RAM
