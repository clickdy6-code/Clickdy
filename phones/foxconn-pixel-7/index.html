Foxconn Pixel 7 at a glance

The Foxconn Pixel 7 was announced in October 2022. It is powered by the Google Tensor G2, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera and 4 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 10.8 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K.

A 4355 mAh battery keeps it running, with 23W wired and 23W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 197 g, measures 8.7 mm in thickness and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 72 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
