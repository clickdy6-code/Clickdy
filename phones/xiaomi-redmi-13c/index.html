Xiaomi Redmi 13C at a glance

The Xiaomi Redmi 13C was announced in November 2023. It has a 6.74-inch IPS LCD display with a 90 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the 13C: MediaTek Helio G85, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 18W wired charging.

The phone measures 8.09 mm in thickness and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 61 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No NFC for contactless payments
