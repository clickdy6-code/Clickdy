Samsung Galaxy S25 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy S25 was announced in February 2025. It has a 6.2-inch LTPO AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 2600 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the All except S25 FE: Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite For Galaxy, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 12 MP.

A 4000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 45W wired and 15W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 162 g, measures 7.2 mm in thickness and runs Android 15.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 85 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
