Sony Xperia acro S at a glance

The Sony Xperia acro S is a smartphone. It has a 4.3-inch TFT display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon S3 MSM8260 1.5 GHz, with up to 1 GB of RAM.

A 1910 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 147 g and runs Android 4.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 25 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
