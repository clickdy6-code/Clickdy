OnePlus 7T at a glance

The OnePlus 7T was announced in September 2019. It has a 6.55-inch AMOLED display with a 90 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 855+, with up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 3800 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 190 g and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 66 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
