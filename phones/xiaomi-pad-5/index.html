Xiaomi Pad 5 at a glance

The Xiaomi Pad 5 was announced in August 2021. It is powered by the Pad 5: Qualcomm Snapdragon 860, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 13 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 8720 mAh battery keeps it running, with 67W wired charging.

The phone weighs 511 g, measures 6.86 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 74 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
