Lenovo Phab 2 Pro at a glance

The Lenovo Phab 2 Pro is a smartphone. It has a 6.4-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the Qualcomm MSM8976 Snapdragon 652, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 64 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 16 MP main camera. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p.

A 4050 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 259 g, measures 10.7 mm in thickness and runs Android 6.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 44 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (4050 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 64 GB
