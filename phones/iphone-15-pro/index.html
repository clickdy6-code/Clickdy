IPhone 15 Pro at a glance

The IPhone 15 Pro was announced in September 2023. It has a 6.1-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 2000 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Apple A17 Pro, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 4 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 12 MP.

A 3274 mAh battery keeps it running, with 40W wired charging.

The phone weighs 187 g, measures 8.25 mm in thickness and runs iOS 17.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 78 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
