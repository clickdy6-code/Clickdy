Samsung Galaxy Z TriFold at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy Z TriFold was announced in December 2025. It is powered by the Qualcomm SM8750-AC Snapdragon 8 Elite, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 200 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 10 MP.

A 5600 mAh battery keeps it running, with 45W wired and 15W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 309 g and runs Android 16.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 87 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
