Vivo Y50t at a glance

The Vivo Y50t was announced in November 2021. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 720G, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 18W wired charging.

The phone weighs 190 g and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 56 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
