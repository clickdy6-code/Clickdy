Meizu U20 at a glance

The Meizu U20 was announced in August 2016. It is powered by the MediaTek Helio P10, with up to 3 GB of RAM and up to 32 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 13 MP main camera. The front camera is 5 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 3260 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 158 g, measures 7.7 mm in thickness and runs Android 6.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 24 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (3260 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
