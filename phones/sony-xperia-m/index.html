Sony Xperia M at a glance

The Sony Xperia M was announced in June 2013. It has a 4-inch TFT display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon S4 Plus MSM8227, with up to 1 GB of RAM and up to 4 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 5 MP main camera. The front camera is 0.3 MP. Video recording goes up to 720p.

A 1700 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 115 g, measures 9.3 mm in thickness and runs Android 4.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 26 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • Battery capacity is on the small side (1700 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 4 GB
  • Only 1 GB RAM
  • Thicker than most phones (9.3 mm)
