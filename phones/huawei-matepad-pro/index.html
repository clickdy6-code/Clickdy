Huawei MatePad Pro at a glance

The Huawei MatePad Pro was announced in December 2019. It is powered by the HiSilicon Kirin 990 5G, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 13 MP main camera. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

The phone weighs 460 g and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 53 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
