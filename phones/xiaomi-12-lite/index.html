Xiaomi 12 Lite at a glance

The Xiaomi 12 Lite was announced in July 2022. It has a 6.55-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm SM7325 Snapdragon 778G 5G, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 108 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4300 mAh battery keeps it running, with 67W wired charging.

The phone weighs 173 g and runs Android 12.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 74 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
