Samsung Galaxy A32 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy A32 was announced in February 2021. It has a 6.4-inch Super AMOLED display with a 90 Hz refresh rate and up to 800 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the 4G: MediaTek Helio G80, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 64 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 20 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 15W wired charging.

The phone weighs 184 g, measures 8.4 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 57 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
