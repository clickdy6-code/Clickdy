Huawei Mate X6 at a glance

The Huawei Mate X6 was announced in November 2024. It has a 7.93-inch LTPO OLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Kirin 9020, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p.

A 5200 mAh battery keeps it running, with 50W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 239 g, measures 4.6 mm in thickness and runs HarmonyOS 4.3.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 89 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
