Sony Xperia 8 at a glance

The Sony Xperia 8 was announced in October 2019. It has a 6-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 630, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 64 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 8 MP.

A 2760 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 170 g, measures 8.1 mm in thickness and runs Android 9.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 38 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2760 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 64 GB
  • Only 4 GB RAM
