Asus ZenFone 8 at a glance

The Asus ZenFone 8 was announced in May 2021. It has a 5.9-inch Super AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 1100 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 888 5G, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 12 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K.

A 4000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 30W wired charging.

The phone weighs 169 g and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 65 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
