LG V20 at a glance

The LG V20 was announced in September 2016. It has a 5.7-inch IPS display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 820, with up to 7 GB of RAM and up to 64 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 16 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 5 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K.

A 3200 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 174 g and runs Android 7.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 43 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Highlights

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (3200 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 52 GB
