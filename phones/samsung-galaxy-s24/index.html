Samsung Galaxy S24 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy S24 was announced in January 2024. It has a 6.2-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 2600 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the S24, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 12 MP.

A 4000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 45W wired and 15W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 168 g, measures 8.6 mm in thickness and runs Android 14.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 83 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
