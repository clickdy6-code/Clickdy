Xiaomi Civi 3 at a glance

The Xiaomi Civi 3 was announced in May 2023. It has a 6.55-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 8200 Ultra, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 67W wired charging.

The phone weighs 173.5 g and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 80 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
