Samsung Galaxy S26 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy S26 was announced in March 2026. It is powered by the S26 & S26+, with up to 1024 GB of storage.

A 4300 mAh battery keeps it running, with 60W wired and 15W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 167 g, measures 7.2 mm in thickness and runs Android 16.
