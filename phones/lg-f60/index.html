LG F60 at a glance

The LG F60 is a smartphone. It has a 4.5-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the Qualcomm MSM8916 Snapdragon 410, with up to 1 GB of RAM and up to 8 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 5 MP main camera. Video recording goes up to 720p@30fps.

A 2100 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 129.6 g and runs Android 4.4.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 28 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2100 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 4 GB
  • Only 1 GB RAM
