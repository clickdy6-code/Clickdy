Oppo Reno3 at a glance

The Oppo Reno3 was announced in March 2020. It is powered by the Reno3: Mediatek MT6779 Helio P90, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 44 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4025 mAh battery keeps it running, with 30W wired charging.

The phone runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 66 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Highlights

  • Generous 12 GB RAM option
