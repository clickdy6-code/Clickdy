Xiaomi Mi 11 Lite at a glance

The Xiaomi Mi 11 Lite was announced in March 2021. It has a 6.55-inch AMOLED display with a 90 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the 4G: Qualcomm SM7150 Snapdragon 732G, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 64 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4250 mAh battery keeps it running, with 33W wired charging.

The phone weighs 157 g and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 68 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
