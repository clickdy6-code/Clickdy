Tecno Spark 9 at a glance

The Tecno Spark 9 was announced in June 2022. It has a 6.6-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the Spark 9T: MediaTek Helio G37, with up to 6 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 2 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 18W wired charging.

The phone runs Android 12.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 46 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
