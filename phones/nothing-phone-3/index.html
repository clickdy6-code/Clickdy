Nothing Phone 3 at a glance

The Nothing Phone 3 was announced in July 2025. It has a 6.67-inch OLED display with a 160 Hz refresh rate and up to 4500 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Qualcomm SM8735, with up to 56 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera. The front camera is 50 MP.

A 5150 mAh battery keeps it running, with 65W wired and 15W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 218 g and runs Android 15.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 85 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
