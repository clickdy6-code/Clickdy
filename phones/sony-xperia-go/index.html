Sony Xperia Go at a glance

The Sony Xperia Go is a smartphone. It is powered by the ST-Ericsson NovaThor U8500, with up to 8 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 5 MP main camera. Video recording goes up to 720p.

A 1305 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone has an IP67 rating, weighs 110 g and runs Android 2.3.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 26 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Highlights

  • IP67 water and dust resistance

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (1305 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 8 GB
