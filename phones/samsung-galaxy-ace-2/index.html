Samsung Galaxy Ace 2 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy Ace 2 was announced in May 2012. It has a 3.8-inch TFT display. It is powered by the ST-Ericsson NovaThor U8500, with up to 4 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 5 MP main camera.

A 1500 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 122 g, measures 10.5 mm in thickness and runs Android 2.3.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 30 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • Battery capacity is on the small side (1500 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 4 GB
  • Thicker than most phones (10.5 mm)
