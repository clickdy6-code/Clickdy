Infinix GT 20 Pro at a glance

The Infinix GT 20 Pro was announced in April 2024. It has a 6.78-inch AMOLED display with a 304 Hz refresh rate and up to 1300 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the MediaTek MT6896Z Dimensity 8200 Ultimate, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 45W wired charging.

The phone weighs 194 g, measures 8.15 mm in thickness and runs Android 14.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 67 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
