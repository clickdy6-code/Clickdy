OPPO Reno4 Lite at a glance

The OPPO Reno4 Lite was announced in September 2020. It has a 6.43-inch AMOLED display. It is powered by the MediaTek MT6779V Helio P95, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 4 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4015 mAh battery keeps it running, with 30W wired charging.

The phone weighs 164 g, measures 7.4 mm in thickness and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 63 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (4015 mAh)
