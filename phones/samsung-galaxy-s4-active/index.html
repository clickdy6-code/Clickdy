Samsung Galaxy S4 Active at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy S4 Active was announced in June 2013. It has a 5-inch TFT display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 600 APQ8064 processor, with up to 2 GB of RAM and up to 32 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 8 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 2 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p.

A 2600 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone runs Android 4.2.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 33 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2600 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 16 GB
  • Only 2 GB RAM
