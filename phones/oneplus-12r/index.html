OnePlus 12R at a glance

The OnePlus 12R was announced in February 2024.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 5500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 100W wired charging.

The phone weighs 207 g and runs Android 14.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 92 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
