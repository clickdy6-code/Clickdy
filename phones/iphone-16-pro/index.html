IPhone 16 Pro at a glance

The IPhone 16 Pro was announced in September 2024. It has a 6.3-inch display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Apple A18 Pro, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 12 MP.

A 3582 mAh battery keeps it running, with 40W wired charging.

The phone weighs 199 g and runs iOS 18.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 77 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
