Sony Xperia XA1 Plus at a glance

The Sony Xperia XA1 Plus is a smartphone. It has a 5.5-inch display.

On the back, it offers a 23 MP main camera. The front camera is 8 MP.

A 3430 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 89 g, measures 8.7 mm in thickness and runs Android 7.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 35 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (3430 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
  • Only 4 GB RAM
