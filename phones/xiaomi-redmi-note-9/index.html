Xiaomi Redmi Note 9 at a glance

The Xiaomi Redmi Note 9 was announced in March 2020. It has a 6.67-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 720G.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 13 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 5020 mAh battery keeps it running, with 33W wired charging.

The phone weighs 209 g, measures 8.8 mm in thickness and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 63 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
