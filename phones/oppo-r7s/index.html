OPPO R7s at a glance

The OPPO R7s is a smartphone. It has a 5.5-inch AMOLED display with a 60 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 615, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 32 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 13 MP main camera. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 3070 mAh battery keeps it running, with 20W wired charging.

The phone weighs 155 g, measures 7 mm in thickness and runs Android 5.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 35 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Only a 60 Hz display
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (3070 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
