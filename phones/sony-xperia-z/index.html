Sony Xperia Z at a glance

The Sony Xperia Z was announced in February 2013. It has a 5-inch TFT display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon S4 Pro APQ8064, with up to 2 GB of RAM and up to 16 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 13.1 MP main camera. The front camera is 2.2 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p.

A 2330 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone has an IP55 rating, weighs 152 g, measures 7.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 12.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 28 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2330 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 16 GB
  • Only 2 GB RAM
