Oppo Find X8 at a glance

The Oppo Find X8 was announced in October 2024. It has a 6.59-inch LTPO AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 4500 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Find X8 / X8 Pro: List of MediaTek systems on chips#Dimensit, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 32 MP.

A 5630 mAh battery keeps it running, with 100W wired and 50W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 193 g, measures 8.8 mm in thickness and runs Android 15.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 93 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
