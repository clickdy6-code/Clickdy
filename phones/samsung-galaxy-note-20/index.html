Samsung Galaxy Note 20 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy Note 20 was announced in August 2020. It has a 6.7-inch Super AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Europe, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 10 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K@24fps.

A 4300 mAh battery keeps it running, with 25W wired and 4.5W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 194 g and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 74 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
