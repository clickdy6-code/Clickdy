LG Optimus Vu II at a glance

The LG Optimus Vu II was announced in September 2012.

On the back, it offers a 8 MP main camera. The front camera is 1.3 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 2150 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 159 g, measures 9.4 mm in thickness and runs Android 4.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 27 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2150 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
  • Only 2 GB RAM
