Oppo Reno at a glance

The Oppo Reno was announced in May 2019. It has a 6.4-inch AMOLED display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 710, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 3765 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 185 g, measures 9.3 mm in thickness and runs Android 9.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 68 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
