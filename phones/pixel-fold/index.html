Pixel Fold at a glance

The Pixel Fold was announced in June 2023. It is powered by the Google Tensor G2, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 9.5 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K.

A 4821 mAh battery keeps it running, with 30W wired charging.

The phone weighs 283 g, measures 12.1 mm in thickness and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 81 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
