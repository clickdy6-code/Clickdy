Samsung Beam i8520 at a glance

The Samsung Beam i8520 is a smartphone. It has a 3.7-inch Super AMOLED display. It is powered by the TI OMAP 3440, with up to 32 GB of RAM and up to 32 GB of storage.

A 1800 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 156 g.
