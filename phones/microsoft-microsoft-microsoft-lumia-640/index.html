Microsoft (Microsoft) Microsoft Lumia 640 at a glance

The Microsoft (Microsoft) Microsoft Lumia 640 was announced in March 2015. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 400 MSM8226/MSM8926, with up to 1 GB of RAM and up to 8 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 8 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 0.9 MP.

A 2500 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 145 g, measures 8.8 mm in thickness and runs Windows Phone 8.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 30 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2500 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 8 GB
