Samsung Galaxy S23 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy S23 was announced in February 2023. It has a 6.1-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 1750 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the S23, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 12 MP.

A 3900 mAh battery keeps it running, with 45W wired and 15W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 168 g, measures 8.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 77 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
