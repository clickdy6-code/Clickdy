OPPO Realme 8 at a glance

The OPPO Realme 8 was announced in March 2021. It has a 6.4-inch Super AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the 8: MediaTek Helio G95, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 64 MP main camera and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 50W wired charging.

The phone weighs 177 g, measures 8.5 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 67 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
