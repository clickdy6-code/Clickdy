Xiaomi 12T at a glance

The Xiaomi 12T is a smartphone. It has a 6.67-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the 12T: MediaTek MT6893 Dimensity 8100-Ultra, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 108 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 20 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 120W wired charging.

The phone weighs 202 g and runs Android 12.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 85 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
