Samsung Galaxy S6 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy S6 was announced in April 2015. It has a 5.1-inch Super AMOLED display. It is powered by the Samsung Exynos 7420 Octa, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 16 MP main camera with optical stabilization. The front camera is 5 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@60fps.

A 2550 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 138 g, measures 6.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 5.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 40 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Highlights

  • Optical image stabilization

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2550 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
