Sony Xperia PRO-I at a glance

The Sony Xperia PRO-I was announced in December 2021. It has a 6.5-inch OLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 888 5G, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 4 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 8 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@24fps.

A 4500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 30W wired charging.

The phone has an IP65 rating, weighs 221 g, measures 8.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 71 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
