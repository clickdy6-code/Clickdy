Samsung Galaxy S21 at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy S21 was announced in January 2021. It has a 6.2-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 1600 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the International: Exynos#Exynos 1000/2000 series, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 10 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K@24fps.

A 4000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 25W wired and 15W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 169 g, measures 8.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 74 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
