Huawei P Smart Z at a glance

The Huawei P Smart Z was announced in May 2019. It has a 6.59-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the Kirin 710F, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 16 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 4000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 10W wired charging.

The phone weighs 196.8 g, measures 8.8 mm in thickness and runs Android 9.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 45 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (4000 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 64 GB
  • Only 4 GB RAM
