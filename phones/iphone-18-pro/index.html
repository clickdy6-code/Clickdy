IPhone 18 Pro at a glance

The IPhone 18 Pro was announced in September 2026. It has a 6.3-inch display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 3000 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Apple A20 Pro, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 2048 GB of storage.

A 4056 mAh battery keeps it running, with 60W wired charging.

The phone weighs 211 g and runs iOS 27.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 82 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
