Huawei P9 at a glance

The Huawei P9 was announced in April 2016. It has a 5.2-inch IPS display. It is powered by the HiSilicon Kirin 955, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 64 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera. The front camera is 8 MP.

A 3000 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 144 g, measures 7 mm in thickness and runs Android 6.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 41 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (3000 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 64 GB
