Foxconn Nokia 3210 at a glance

The Foxconn Nokia 3210 was announced in May 2024. It has a 2.4-inch TFT display. It is powered by the Unisoc T107.

On the back, it offers a 2 MP main camera.

A 1450 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 87 g and measures 13.1 mm in thickness.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 29 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (1450 mAh)
  • Thicker than most phones (13.1 mm)
