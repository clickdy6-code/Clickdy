Foxconn Nokia 1 Plus at a glance

The Foxconn Nokia 1 Plus is a smartphone. It has a 5.45-inch IPS display. It is powered by the MediaTek MT6739WW, with up to 1 GB of RAM and up to 8 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 8 MP main camera. The front camera is 5 MP.

A 2500 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone runs Android 9.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 26 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2500 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 8 GB
