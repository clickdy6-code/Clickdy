OnePlus 9 at a glance

The OnePlus 9 was announced in March 2021. It has a 6.55-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 888, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 48 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 4 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K.

A 4500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 65W wired and 15W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 192 g, measures 8.7 mm in thickness and runs Android 11.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 85 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
