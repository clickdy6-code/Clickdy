Microsoft Lumia 950 at a glance

The Microsoft Lumia 950 was announced in November 2015. It has a 5.2-inch AMOLED display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 808, with up to 3 GB of RAM and up to 32 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 20 MP main camera with optical stabilization. The front camera is 5 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K.

A 3000 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 150 g and measures 8.2 mm in thickness.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 36 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider
