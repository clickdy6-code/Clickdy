Gigaset ME at a glance

The Gigaset ME is a smartphone.

On the back, it offers a 16 MP main camera. The front camera is 8 MP.

A 3000 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 160 g and runs Android 5.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 29 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (3000 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
