Honor Play 20 at a glance

The Honor Play 20 was announced in April 2021. It has a 6.52-inch IPS LCD display. It is powered by the Play 20/5T: Unisoc T610, with up to 8 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 13 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 5 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 10W wired charging.

The phone weighs 198 g and runs Android 10.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 46 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
