Xiaomi Poco M6 Pro at a glance

The Xiaomi Poco M6 Pro was announced in January 2024. It has a 6.67-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 1300 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the MediaTek Helio G99 Ultra, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 64 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p@30fps.

A 5000 mAh battery keeps it running, with 67W wired charging.

The phone weighs 179 g and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 70 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
