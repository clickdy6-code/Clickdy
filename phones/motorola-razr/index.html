Motorola Razr at a glance

The Motorola Razr was announced in November 2019. It has a 6.2-inch OLED display. It is powered by the 4G: Qualcomm Snapdragon 710, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 16 MP main camera and 2 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 5 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 2510 mAh battery keeps it running, with 45W wired charging.

The phone weighs 205 g, measures 6.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 9.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 61 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
