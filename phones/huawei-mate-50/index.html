Huawei Mate 50 at a glance

The Huawei Mate 50 was announced in September 2022. It has a 6.7-inch OLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm SM8425 Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 4G, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 13 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4460 mAh battery keeps it running, with 66W wired and 50W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 206 g, measures 8 mm in thickness and runs HarmonyOS 3.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 79 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
