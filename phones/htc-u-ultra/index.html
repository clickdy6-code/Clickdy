HTC U Ultra at a glance

The HTC U Ultra is a smartphone. It has a 5.7-inch display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 821, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera with optical stabilization. The front camera is 16 MP.

A 3000 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 170 g, measures 8 mm in thickness and runs Android 12.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 36 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
