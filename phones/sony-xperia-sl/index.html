Sony Xperia SL at a glance

The Sony Xperia SL was announced in September 2012. It has a 4.3-inch TFT display.

On the back, it offers a 12.1 MP main camera. The front camera is 1.3 MP. Video recording goes up to 1080p.

A 1750 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 144 g and measures 10.6 mm in thickness.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 28 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (1750 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 26 GB
  • Only 1 GB RAM
