Xiaomi 13 Lite at a glance

The Xiaomi 13 Lite was announced in September 2022. It has a 6.55-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the Qualcomm SM7450-AB Snapdragon 7 Gen 1, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera and 4 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K@30fps.

A 4500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 67W wired charging.

The phone weighs 171 g, measures 7.23 mm in thickness and runs Android 12.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 74 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
