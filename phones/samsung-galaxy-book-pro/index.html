Samsung Galaxy Book Pro at a glance

The Samsung Galaxy Book Pro was announced in April 2021. It has a 3.3-inch AMOLED display. It is powered by the Intel Core i5 1135G7, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

The phone measures 11.5 mm in thickness.
