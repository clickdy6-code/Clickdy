IPhone XS at a glance

The IPhone XS was announced in September 2018. It has a 5.85-inch display. It is powered by the Apple A12 Bionic, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera. The front camera is 7 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K.

A 2658 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 177 g and runs iOS 12.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 40 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2658 mAh)
  • Only 4 GB RAM
