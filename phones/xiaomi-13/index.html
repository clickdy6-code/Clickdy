Xiaomi 13 at a glance

The Xiaomi 13 was announced in December 2022. It has a 6.36-inch AMOLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 1100 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the 13/13 Pro: Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 512 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K@24fps.

A 4500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 120W wired and 50W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 185 g, measures 7.98 mm in thickness and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 83 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
