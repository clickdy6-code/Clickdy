HONOR X9b at a glance

The HONOR X9b was announced in October 2023. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 1, with up to 20 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 108 MP main camera and 3 rear cameras in total. The front camera is 16 MP.

A 5800 mAh battery keeps it running, with 35W wired charging.

The phone weighs 185 g and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the upper mid-range group, with a Clickdy Score of 72 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
