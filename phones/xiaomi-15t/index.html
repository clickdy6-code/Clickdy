Xiaomi 15T at a glance

The Xiaomi 15T was announced in September 2025. It has a 6.83-inch AMOLED display with a 144 Hz refresh rate. It is powered by the 15T: MediaTek Dimensity 8400-Ultra, with up to 12 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 5 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K.

A 5500 mAh battery keeps it running, with 90W wired and 50W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 194 g, measures 7.5 mm in thickness and runs Android 15.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 86 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
