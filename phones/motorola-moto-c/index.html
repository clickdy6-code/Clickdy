Motorola Moto C at a glance

The Motorola Moto C is a smartphone. It is powered by the Moto C: MediaTek MT6737M, with up to 2 GB of RAM and up to 16 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 8 MP main camera. Video recording goes up to 720p.

A 2350 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 154 g, measures 9 mm in thickness and runs Android 7.0.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 29 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • Battery capacity is on the small side (2350 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 16 GB
  • Only 2 GB RAM
  • Thicker than most phones (9 mm)
