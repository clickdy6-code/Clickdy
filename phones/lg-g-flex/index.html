LG G Flex at a glance

The LG G Flex was announced in November 2013. It has a 6-inch OLED display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 800, with up to 2 GB of RAM and up to 32 GB of storage.

A 3500 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 177 g, measures 7.9 mm in thickness and runs Android 4.2.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 41 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Things to consider

  • Battery capacity is on the small side (3500 mAh)
  • Base storage is only 32 GB
  • Only 2 GB RAM
