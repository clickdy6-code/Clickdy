LG V40 ThinQ at a glance

The LG V40 ThinQ was announced in October 2018. It has a 6.4-inch OLED display. It is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 845, with up to 6 GB of RAM and up to 128 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 8 MP.

A 3300 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone runs Android 8.1.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 50 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.

Highlights

Things to consider

  • No 5G support
  • No NFC for contactless payments
