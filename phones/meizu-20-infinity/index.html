Meizu 20 Infinity at a glance

The Meizu 20 Infinity was announced in June 2023. It has a 6.79-inch LTPO OLED display with a 120 Hz refresh rate and up to 1800 nits of peak brightness. It is powered by the Qualcomm SM8550-AB Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, with up to 16 GB of RAM and up to 1024 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 50 MP main camera with optical stabilization and 3 rear cameras in total, including a telephoto lens. The front camera is 32 MP. Video recording goes up to 8K.

A 4800 mAh battery keeps it running, with 65W wired and 50W wireless charging.

The phone weighs 215 g and runs Android 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the flagship-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 89 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
