Foxconn (on contract) IPhone 11 at a glance

The Foxconn (on contract) IPhone 11 was announced in September 2019. It is powered by the A13 Bionic, with up to 4 GB of RAM and up to 256 GB of storage.

On the back, it offers a 12 MP main camera with optical stabilization. The front camera is 12 MP. Video recording goes up to 4K.

A 3110 mAh battery keeps it running.

The phone weighs 194 g, measures 8.3 mm in thickness and runs iOS 13.

On paper, its specifications place it in the entry-level group, with a Clickdy Score of 46 out of 100. This score is based only on the spec sheet, not on lab tests.
