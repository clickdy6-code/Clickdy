What is Title Case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of each word. Useful for headings and titles.

What is camelCase?

camelCase joins words together and capitalizes each word after the first. Common in JavaScript.

What is snake_case?

snake_case separates words with underscores. Common in Python and variable naming.
