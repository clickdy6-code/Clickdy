Important Islamic finance note

This is an educational estimate, not a religious ruling. Nisab standards, jewelry, investment treatment, debt deductions and the calculation date can differ between scholarly opinions and schools of thought. Consult a qualified scholar for your circumstances.

What is Nisab?

Nisab is the minimum qualifying wealth threshold. Common reference weights are approximately 87.48g of gold or 612.36g of silver. Their local monetary values change with market prices.

How is Zakat calculated?

A common basic calculation is 2.5% of net eligible wealth when the relevant Nisab and lunar-year conditions are fulfilled.

What about liabilities?

Only liabilities deductible under the scholarly method you follow should be entered. Long-term debt treatment may differ from immediate amounts due.
