Important conversion notes
Selectable-text PDFs
Best results are achieved with normal text-based PDF files.
Scanned documents
Scanned pages are images. OCR is required to recognize their text; this version does not claim to convert image-only pages into editable text.
Formatting
Complex tables, images, columns, exact fonts and original page layouts may need manual adjustment in Word.
